Store Policies

Orders are not placed or confirmed through WhatsApp chat. Staff can answer availability questions, voucher checks, and order-status follow-ups, but checkout must happen through the store's supported sales flow.

Returns and Refunds

Sealed products may be returned only if they remain unopened and are reported promptly with proof of purchase. Singles are generally final sale unless the item was materially misdescribed by store staff.

Pickup and Hold Policy

Inventory availability can change quickly. Staff may confirm whether an item appears available, but stock is not guaranteed until payment is completed through the store's supported checkout process. Temporary holds, if offered, require staff approval.

Event Policy

WhatsApp can provide event details, but event registration is not completed in chat unless a dedicated registration workflow is enabled later. Players should confirm seat availability before traveling for limited-capacity events.

Voucher Policy

Voucher and promo validity depends on the code, product category, and expiry window. Discounts may exclude preorders, allocations, and selected premium sealed items.
