# Mox & Lotus SG

![](/images/MoxLotus-AboutUs1.png)

At Mox & Lotus, we believe in crafting the best gaming experience possible, which is why we put immense focus on customer service, as well as the quality and variety of our offerings. As a customer-oriented company, Mox & Lotus is committed to becoming the best place in the world to buy Magic cards and other collectibles. The values we uphold are fairness, respect, integrity, and honesty, so no matter where you are, you can feel confident you will be well taken care of.  
  
Chapman Sim has been playing Magic: The Gathering since 1994, making it a lifelong passion. In 2020, Chapman Sim founded Mox & Lotus. Through his work with Hasbro and Wizards of the Coast in the past decade, Chapman has brought coverage from over 70 events to millions of fans around the world. In addition to finishing in the Top 8 of four Grand Prix events and being a two-time National Champion, he has also competed in over 20 Pro Tours and was the first player in Asia to complete Pokemon Go.
